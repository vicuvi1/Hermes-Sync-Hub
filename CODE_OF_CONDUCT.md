# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, patient, and mindful that contributors have different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Protect other people's privacy and never publish their credentials or personal data.
- Avoid harassment, threats, sexualized content, discrimination, and sustained disruption.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens the community or the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and recurrence.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
